2. 국외 이전

질문을 보낼 때마다 질문 문장과 정답 단어가 네트워크로 Anthropic(미국)에 전송되어 대답을 만드는 데만 쓰입니다. 광고는 Google(미국 등)이 표시합니다. 원하지 않으면 앱을 이용하지 않거나 지울 수 있습니다. 광고 맞춤 설정은 기기 설정(iOS: 개인정보 보호 및 보안 › 추적, Android: 설정 › 개인정보 보호 › 광고)에서 끌 수 있습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

Guess Me has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

What we process

  • A random device identifier created on first launch — to keep your coins, current game, and collection (our server, South Korea).
  • Game records: questions, Peeko's answers, guesses, coin history, timestamps — to run the game, show past conversations, prevent ad-reward abuse, and improve answers (our server, South Korea).
  • Question text and the secret word are sent to Anthropic, PBC (USA) to generate Peeko's answer. Your device identifier is not sent.
  • Advertising information such as the advertising ID is collected by Google AdMob to show ads and grant ad rewards.

Please don't type personal information into questions. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Retention and deletion

We keep game records while the service runs and delete them promptly on request, or when the service ends.
